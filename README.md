**Keep Onward!** is a fast-paced 3D endless runner where the only real goal is to keep moving forward.

Run across a floating medieval bridge, dodge incoming obstacles, collect coins and apples, grab hearts, and try to survive for as long as possible.

---

## Gameplay

The game is simple to pick up, but staying alive is another story.

-  Run forward through the level
-  Move left, right, up, down to avoid obstacles
-  Collect coins
-  Collect apples
-  Pick up hearts to keep yourself alive
-  Dodge obstacles flying toward you
-  React to obstacles with different speeds and rotations
-  Try to survive and get the most score as possible

Every run is slightly different thanks to randomly placed collectibles and obstacles.

---

##  Obstacles

You'll encounter different obstacles throughout the run, including:

-  Wooden crates
-  Barrels
-  Moving carts
-  Cannons
-  Treasure chests
-  Wooden/stone fences

Some obstacles stay on the path like fences.

Others?

**They come flying at you.**

Their movement speed and rotation can vary, meaning you can't simply memorize the level and expect to survive.

---

##  Lives

You start with a limited number of lives.

Getting hit by an obstacle costs a life, so choosing when move or take a risk actually matters.

Grab heart pickups along the way to keep the run going.

---

##  Collectibles

###  Coins
Collect coins scattered throughout the level.

Their positions are randomized, so you'll have to constantly adjust your movement.


###  Apples
Apples are placed around the path as additional collectibles and they increase the speed of the player.

Sometimes they're easy to grab.

Sometimes they're sitting right next to an obstacle.


###  Hearts
Heart pickups can restore a life and give you another chance to continue your run.

---

##  Randomized Gameplay

One of the main ideas behind **Keep Onward!** is keeping each run unpredictable.

Obstacle and collectible positions are randomized across the level, while incoming obstacles can have different movement speeds and rotations.

This means the player has to react to what's happening instead of simply memorizing a fixed route.

The game uses a simple, colorful low-poly style with a medieval/fantasy theme.

The UI elements like menu button sprites and the game cover page was made using AI.
